Begin by re-reading the world, per [`SKILL.md`](SKILL.md). Then:

1. Cut the work into vertical slices per the `slicing` skill.
2. For every slice, answer "what can be demonstrated when this is done?" A slice with no answer is a layer. Recut it.
3. Write `.capstan/effort/plan.md` holding the slices and the blocking edges between them. This graph is yours and it never leaves the effort folder.
4. Agree the test seams here, in the spec, not during the build. A Builder handed no seam will pick one.
5. Update `.capstan/effort/CLAIM.md`, including the `next` line, then post the gate-2 brief. End the run.

**Done when** every slice in `plan.md` carries a demonstration, a test seam, and its blocking edges.
